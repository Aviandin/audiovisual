# Audiovisualizer

Audiovisualizer turns sound into colorful, reactive visuals. Capture audio playing on your device or use your microphone, then customize the look to suit your setup.

## Features

- **Choose your audio source:** visualize sound from your device or from a microphone.
- **Dynamic RGB colors:** enable a shifting RGB effect and adjust its speed.
- **Static colors:** choose a steady color instead of a changing RGB effect.
- **Customizable settings:** tune the visualizer to the look and feel you want.

## Getting started

1. Download and run [`Audiovisual-Setup.exe`](./Audiovisual-Setup.exe).
2. Open Audiovisualizer.
3. Select whether to capture audio from your device or microphone.
4. Choose your color mode and adjust the available settings.

## Customize your visuals

Use the settings to switch between animated RGB colors and a static color. When using RGB, adjust the speed to make the color changes faster or slower.
